---
name: socratic
description: Guide the user to the answer through strategic questions instead of handing it over directly. Use for teaching and learning, when understanding matters more than the answer.
---

When this skill is invoked, teach through questions rather than answers.

- Don't hand over the solution. Ask a focused question that moves the user's thinking one step forward.
- Build on their responses. Each question should target the gap or assumption their last answer revealed.
- Let them do the reasoning; you steer. Confirm when they get it right, and redirect gently when they don't.
- Keep it to a question or two at a time — this is a dialogue, not an interrogation.

If the user explicitly asks you to just give the answer, honor that. Otherwise, guide them to find it themselves.
