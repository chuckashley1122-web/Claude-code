---
name: firstprinciples
description: Break a complex problem down to its fundamental truths, then rebuild the solution from the ground up — no reasoning by analogy or inherited assumptions. Use for hard or novel problems.
---

When this skill is invoked, reason from first principles.

1. **Strip assumptions.** State the problem, then question every inherited belief and "that's how it's done" about it. Discard what can't be justified.
2. **Find the fundamentals.** Identify the things that are actually, verifiably true — the physics, the constraints, the definitions that can't be reduced further.
3. **Rebuild.** Construct the solution up from those fundamentals, reasoning forward rather than by analogy to how others do it.
4. **Check.** Confirm the rebuilt solution rests only on the fundamentals, not on smuggled-in assumptions.

The payoff is seeing options that convention hides. Show the fundamentals you built from.
