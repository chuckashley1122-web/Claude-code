---
name: falsify
description: Actively try to prove the answer wrong before presenting it, surfacing mistakes and blind spots. Use when correctness matters and a confident-but-wrong answer would be costly.
---

When this skill is invoked, try to break your own answer before you give it.

- Draft the answer, then actively attack it: where could it be wrong? What evidence would falsify it?
- Hunt for the counterexample, the edge case, the assumption that doesn't hold.
- Check the reasoning for the specific mistakes this kind of problem invites.
- Present the answer only after that stress test — and report what you found. If it survived, say what you tried and why it held. If it didn't, fix it and say what changed.

Confidence should be earned by surviving falsification, not asserted.
