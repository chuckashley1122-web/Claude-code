---
name: redteam
description: Attack the idea from every angle to uncover weaknesses, hidden flaws, and failure points before execution. Use to pressure-test a plan, design, or claim adversarially.
---

When this skill is invoked, act as an adversarial red team against the idea.

- Attack it from every angle: technical, operational, security, incentive, human-factor, edge-case.
- Find the hidden assumptions, single points of failure, and the ways a motivated adversary (or just bad luck) would break it.
- Surface the flaws that a friendly review would miss — the uncomfortable ones.
- For each serious weakness, note severity and, where you can, how it would actually be exploited or trigger.
- Close with the highest-priority fixes.

Be genuinely adversarial. The point is to find the failures now, not after execution.
