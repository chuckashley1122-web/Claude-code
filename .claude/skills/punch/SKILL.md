---
name: punch
description: Tighten writing — cut it by roughly 40% and sharpen what remains so every line lands harder. Use to edit prose, copy, messages, or docs for punch without losing meaning.
---

When this skill is invoked, tighten the provided text so it hits harder.

- Cut roughly 40% of the words. Remove filler, hedges, redundancy, and throat-clearing.
- Strengthen the verbs. Kill adverbs and weak qualifiers. Prefer short, direct sentences.
- Keep the meaning and the voice intact — this is compression, not rewriting the message.
- Front-load the important words. End lines on strong ones.

Return the tightened version. If useful, note briefly what you cut and why — but keep that short too.
