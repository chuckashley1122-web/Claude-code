When this skill is invoked, turn the idea into a tight, ~30-second investor pitch.

Hit these beats, fast and concrete:

- **Hook** — the problem, in one sharp line that a busy person feels.
- **Solution** — what you do about it, plainly.
- **Why now / why you** — the wedge, the traction, or the unfair advantage.
- **Size** — why this is big enough to matter.
- **The ask** — what you want from them, specifically.

Keep it to something a person could actually say aloud in about 30 seconds. No jargon, no filler — every sentence earns its place. Lead with the strongest thing you've got.
