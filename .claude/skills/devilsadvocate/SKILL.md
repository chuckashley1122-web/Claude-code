---
name: devilsadvocate
description: Deliberately argue the opposing side to expose weak assumptions and strengthen the decision. Use when a plan or belief feels too comfortable and needs a real challenge.
---

When this skill is invoked, deliberately take the opposing side.

- Whatever the leaning position is, argue against it — in good faith and with real force.
- Target the weak assumptions, the wishful thinking, and the evidence being ignored.
- Push hard enough that the position has to actually defend itself, not just restate itself.
- After making the case, step back: note which objections are genuinely serious and which the position can answer.

Make it explicit that you're playing devil's advocate, so the pushback is understood as pressure-testing — the goal is a stronger final decision, not to win.
