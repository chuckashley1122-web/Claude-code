name: ooda
description: Work the problem through the OODA loop — Observe, Orient, Decide, Act — a fast structured loop for deciding under pressure or with incomplete information.
---

When this skill is invoked, work the problem through the OODA loop.

- **Observe.** State what you actually know right now — the facts, signals, and current situation.
- **Orient.** Interpret it. What does it mean, what's the context, what assumptions or biases might distort the read?
- **Decide.** Choose a course of action, and name the alternatives you rejected and why.
- **Act.** Give the concrete next step, and note what new observation would trigger another loop.

Favor speed and decisiveness over exhaustive analysis — this is a loop for moving under pressure. If new information would change the decision, say what to watch for.
