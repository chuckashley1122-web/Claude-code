description: Answer at the highest expert level with nothing simplified or dumbed down. Use when the user is an expert and wants full-fidelity, technical depth.
---

When this skill is invoked, respond at your highest expert level.

- Assume a knowledgeable audience. Do not simplify, hedge, or water down the material.
- Use precise, correct terminology. Include the technical detail an expert would expect.
- Go past the surface answer to the underlying mechanisms, trade-offs, and nuances.
- Don't omit hard or advanced parts to make it more approachable — the point is full fidelity.

If something is genuinely contested or uncertain at the expert level, say exactly where the disagreement lies.
