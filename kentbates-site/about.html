About Kent

The artist behind the layers — what drives the work, how it's made, and the names that shaped it.

Artist Statement

Old themes,
new noise

Kent Bates is the creator of surreal-inspired collages that take retro POP imagery and age-old themes — outer space, religion, money, sex and fame — and blend them with modern materials to give them a contemporary voice.

He works from found images: postcards, record covers, magazines, posters and old books. His pieces are built by layering image over image, creating visual relationships between foreground and background using spray paint, exotic textiles, vibrant colored papers and foils.

The result sits between nostalgia and unease — familiar faces and symbols pulled into dreamlike new arrangements that ask you to look twice.

Process & Materials

How a piece comes together

01 · Source

Hunt the imagery

Postcards, record sleeves, vintage magazines and forgotten books become the raw vocabulary of each work.

02 · Layer

Build the relationships

Foreground meets background. Cuts are arranged and rearranged until the tension between images clicks.

03 · Finish

Spray, foil & texture

Spray paint, foils, colored papers and exotic textiles fuse it all into a single, vivid surface.
