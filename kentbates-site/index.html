The Artist

Pop nostalgia,
with a contemporary voice

Kent Bates makes surreal collages from retro POP imagery, exploring age-old themes — space, religion, money, sex and fame — through a modern lens.

Working with found images, postcards, record covers and old books, he layers foreground against background using spray paint, vibrant papers, foils and exotic textiles. The result is loud, dreamlike and unmistakably his.
