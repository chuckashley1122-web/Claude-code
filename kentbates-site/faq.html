Questions & answers

Everything you need to know about buying, shipping, authenticity and commissioning work. Still stuck? Get in touch →

How do I buy an original or print?

Limited prints and merch can be bought directly through the Shop. Originals are one of a kind — use the inquire button or email KB@KENTBATES.com and Kent will confirm availability, price and shipping, usually within 1–2 days.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. Prints and merch ship worldwide. Original works ship worldwide too, fully insured — international shipping for large originals is quoted per piece so it's packed and protected properly.

What is your return policy?

Prints and merch can be returned within 14 days in original condition for a refund or exchange. Original one-of-a-kind works and custom commissions are final sale. Anything damaged in transit is replaced or refunded in full — just send a photo.

Are works signed and authenticated?

Every original is signed and ships with a certificate of authenticity. Prints are signed and numbered within their edition, so you always know exactly what you own.

Do you take commissions?

Yes — custom collages are made for collectors, brands and spaces. Typical turnaround is 3–6 weeks depending on size and complexity.

To start, share your theme, size, budget and timeline via the contact form.

Are pieces framed?

Originals can be supplied framed or unframed — just ask. Prints ship unframed (rolled or flat) and are sized to fit standard frames.

Still have a question?

Kent answers every message personally. Ask about a specific piece, a commission, or shipping to your country.
