Pool service business software and equipment, compared

Pool service is a route business. Revenue comes from recurring stops, margin comes from how many of them a technician completes in a day, and cash flow comes from billing that runs without anyone chasing it. Most of the software below exists to attack one of those three things.

There is also gear here, because water testing and equipment choices shape the service itself — what you can diagnose at the pool, what you have to come back for, and what you can charge for. Every page covers what the product does, who it suits, and where it falls short.

How this list is put together

Entries are described from vendor documentation, published pricing, product manuals and specification sheets. Pool service software pricing is often structured per stop or per technician, and the table reflects that rather than reducing everything to one figure.

The selection covers the real decisions in this trade:

  • Four purpose-built pool service platforms, which handle routes, water chemistry logging and recurring billing natively.
  • One general trades platform, for companies doing repair and construction work alongside cleaning routes.
  • One pool-specific office platform aimed at companies with both service and retail sides.
  • Two water-testing options at very different price points, since testing accuracy underpins everything else.
  • Two pieces of equipment that regularly come up as recommendations to customers.
  • No product was included or excluded because of an affiliate relationship.
