What it does

CV-600 is a concentrated sequestering agent and phosphate remover used to control scale formation and strip the phosphates that feed algae. Orenda's approach ties dosing to the Langelier Saturation Index and measured phosphate levels rather than a fixed schedule, which makes it a chemistry program more than a single product.

Who it suits

Techs working hard fill water, high-calcium pools, and persistent algae accounts where chlorine alone keeps losing. Companies that want to sell water quality as a service rather than react to green pools benefit most.

Where it falls short

It demands testing you may not currently do — phosphate testing and LSI calculation — so the product implies buying more test equipment and learning the method. Used without that discipline it is expensive water. Cost per gallon is high relative to generic sequestering agents, and justifying it to a price-sensitive homeowner takes an explanation most customers do not want. It is preventive, so results are slow and undramatic, which makes it a hard upsell. It is not a substitute for correcting the underlying water balance.
