What it does

PoolTrac focuses on route execution and accountability: scheduling, chemistry and dosing logs, photo documentation, and GPS-verified stop records showing where and when a technician actually was. For an owner who cannot ride every route, that verification is the point of the product.

Who it suits

Owners managing technicians they cannot directly supervise, companies with disputes over skipped stops, and operations wanting a defensible record of service delivery.

Where it falls short

It is a smaller product with a smaller company behind it, so the integration ecosystem and third-party accounting connections are thinner and feature development is slower. Repair and inventory management are minimal. GPS verification can be a morale problem if introduced badly — techs read it as surveillance, and that is a management conversation the software will not have for you. Reporting depth is limited, and commercial account requirements are not addressed.
