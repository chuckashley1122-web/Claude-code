Paythepoolman: what it does and where it falls short

Named after the problem it solves — a billing tool first, with routes attached.

Category
Software — Pool service billing and route management
Starting price
From approx. $30–$50/month depending on plan
Best for
Small pool companies whose main problem is getting paid on time
Not a good fit for
Companies needing deep chemistry or oversight tooling

What it does

Paythepoolman concentrates on the cash cycle. Customers are set up on recurring monthly service billing, cards and bank accounts are stored, invoices generate and charge themselves, and the owner gets a clear view of who has paid and who has not — which for a small route business is usually the difference between a healthy month and a stressful one.

Around the billing it handles routes and stop lists, service records with readings and chemicals, work orders for repairs, and customer communication. It is a lighter product than the larger platforms, and priced accordingly, which makes it a reasonable first step for a company moving off a spreadsheet and a personal payment app.

Who it suits

This suits a small operator whose bookkeeping is the bottleneck.

  • One to a few technician companies still invoicing manually or collecting payment ad hoc.
  • Owners spending evenings chasing monthly payments.
  • Businesses wanting recurring card and bank collection without a large monthly software cost.
  • Route companies with straightforward service and little repair work.

Where it falls short

Lower price means a narrower product.

  • Chemistry features are basic. There is no dosing engine or chemistry analysis of the kind the specialist platforms offer.
  • Technician oversight and accountability tooling is minimal, so it does less for you as headcount grows.
  • Smaller vendor, smaller ecosystem — fewer integrations and less third-party support than the market leaders.
  • Reporting is limited to the essentials; route profitability and chemical cost analysis are not its territory.
  • Repair and equipment replacement job management is light, so companies with a real service department will outgrow it.

Before you buy

Work out what late and missed payments actually cost you per year. If that number is large and everything else about your operation is fine, this is a well-targeted purchase.

Check the payment processing rates, since the whole value here runs through collection.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
