What it does

Jobber provides requests, quoting, scheduling, job tracking, invoicing, and online payment with a customer hub. For pool companies it earns its place when the business is really a repair, renovation, or equipment installation operation that also happens to run some routes.

Who it suits

Pool businesses weighted toward project and repair work, companies also doing adjacent outdoor services, and anyone who wants mature quoting and invoicing over pool-specific logging.

Where it falls short

It has no pool-specific features at all: no chemistry logging, no dosing records, no chemical usage tracking, no automated post-service report with test results. Route optimization for thirty stops a day is not what its scheduler was designed for. Pool companies adopting it typically end up tracking water chemistry in a separate app or on paper, which defeats much of the purpose. Per-user pricing on higher tiers adds up.
