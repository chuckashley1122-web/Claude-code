Jobber: what it does and where it falls short

Not a pool product — which is exactly why it suits companies doing repair and renovation work.

Category
Software — General trades quoting and job management
Starting price
From approx. $39/month (published tiers)
Best for
Pool companies whose revenue leans toward repairs, renovations and installations
Not a good fit for
High-volume recurring cleaning routes

What it does

Jobber runs work as requests, quotes, jobs and invoices. Its quoting is the strongest part: templated line items, optional extras the customer can approve themselves, online acceptance with signature, and automatic follow-up when a quote goes quiet. For pool companies selling heater replacements, pump and filter upgrades, resurfacing or equipment automation, that is the part of the business where money is won or lost.

It also covers scheduling with a drag-and-drop calendar, route planning, a technician app with forms and photos, recurring job support for maintenance visits, a client portal, automated invoicing and reminders, and a well-regarded QuickBooks Online sync. None of it is pool-specific, which is both the limitation and, for repair-led businesses, the point.

Who it suits

This fits a pool business that looks more like a contractor than a route.

  • Companies where equipment replacement, renovation and repair work is the majority of revenue.
  • Businesses doing pool work alongside other trades such as landscaping or outdoor construction.
  • Owners who want strong quoting and customer-facing communication more than chemistry logging.
  • Shops already on QuickBooks Online wanting the bookkeeping automated.

Where it falls short

There is nothing about pools in it, and that is a real cost for route businesses.

  • No water chemistry or dosing features at all. Logging readings means bending custom fields, and there is no dosing calculation or chemistry history.
  • No pool-specific service report — the photo-and-readings report customers now expect from pool companies has to be improvised.
  • Route management is adequate for scheduled jobs but not built for dense daily cleaning routes with frequent reassignment.
  • Chemical cost tracking per pool, a standard feature in pool platforms, is not available.
  • Per-user seat limits on lower tiers bite quickly once you add seasonal help.

Before you buy

Split your revenue between recurring cleaning and repair or construction. If cleaning dominates, buy a pool-specific platform instead; if repairs dominate, Jobber's strengths are where your money is.

Some companies run both — a pool platform for routes and a trades platform for projects. Price that combination before dismissing it.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
