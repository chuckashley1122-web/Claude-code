What it does

The K-2006C is the reference wet test kit for pool water, using FAS-DPD titration to measure free and combined chlorine precisely at high concentrations, plus pH, total alkalinity, calcium hardness, and cyanuric acid. Unlike strips or colorimetric comparators, titration gives a numeric result you can actually dose against, and it does not saturate out at high chlorine levels.

Who it suits

Any pool professional who needs to know actual chlorine and CYA numbers rather than approximate them — which is anyone managing chlorine relative to stabilizer, diagnosing combined chlorine, or defending a water balance decision.

Where it falls short

It is slow. Running a full panel takes several minutes of careful drop counting, which is a real problem on a thirty-stop route where competitors are reading a strip in ten seconds. Reagents expire and need replacing, so there is an ongoing cost and a shelf-life discipline. Drop counting is error-prone when rushed or in bright sun, and the CYA turbidity test is genuinely subjective. The kit is bulky and the reagent bottles leak if tipped in a truck. It measures salt only with an add-on.
