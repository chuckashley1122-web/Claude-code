What it does

Pool Office Manager covers route scheduling and service logging alongside repair job tracking, estimates, invoicing, recurring billing, and QuickBooks synchronization. The distinguishing feature is treating repair work as a first-class workflow rather than an afterthought bolted onto a route app.

Who it suits

Pool companies whose revenue is genuinely split between recurring route service and repair or equipment replacement work, and owners who want one system instead of a route app plus a generic CRM.

Where it falls short

The interface is less polished than Skimmer's and the technician mobile experience is noticeably rougher, which matters when techs use it forty times a day. Route optimization is weaker. Onboarding takes longer because the product covers more ground, and smaller route-only operations will pay for repair features they never touch. Customer-facing service report presentation is less refined, which costs you the retention benefit competitors lean on.
