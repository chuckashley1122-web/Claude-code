Pool Office Manager: what it does and where it falls short

Its strongest argument is accounting integration — service data landing in QuickBooks without retyping.

Category
Software — Pool service scheduling and billing
Starting price
From approx. $50–$80/month depending on plan and user count
Best for
Established pool companies running QuickBooks that want service and accounting joined up
Not a good fit for
Companies wanting the most polished technician mobile experience

What it does

Pool Office Manager handles the operational core: recurring service scheduling and route assignment, technician job completion with chemical and reading logs, repair work orders, and invoicing with recurring billing and online payment. A customer portal lets clients see service history and pay invoices themselves.

The reason companies choose it over flashier competitors is usually the QuickBooks integration. Pool service generates a high volume of small recurring invoices, and having those flow into accounting correctly — rather than being re-entered or imported badly — saves a bookkeeper real hours every month. For an established company with an existing accounting process, that can outweigh a nicer technician app.

Who it suits

This suits a company where the office matters as much as the route.

  • Established pool companies with a bookkeeper and an existing QuickBooks workflow.
  • Businesses with high recurring invoice volume where accounting sync errors are costing time.
  • Companies wanting a customer portal for history and self-service payment.
  • Operations mixing recurring service with regular repair work.

Where it falls short

It is office-strong and field-average.

  • The technician mobile experience is less refined than the newer route-first apps, and technicians notice interface friction every single stop.
  • Chemistry automation and dosing guidance are basic compared with the chemistry-focused platforms.
  • The interface generally looks and feels older than competitors, which affects training time for new staff.
  • Route optimisation is present but not a standout feature.
  • Smaller vendor than the category leader, with a correspondingly smaller integration ecosystem.

Before you buy

Test the QuickBooks sync with your own chart of accounts and invoice structure during a trial. That is the feature you would be buying it for, so verify it rather than assuming.

Have a technician — not you — use the mobile app for a full day before deciding.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
