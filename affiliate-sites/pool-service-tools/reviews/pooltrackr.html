Pooltrackr: what it does and where it falls short

The one here that takes retail seriously, which matters if you also sell across a counter.

Category
Software — Pool business management including retail
Starting price
From approx. $30 per user/month (published tiers)
Best for
Pool companies with a shop or retail side alongside service
Not a good fit for
Pure route cleaning companies with no retail or construction work

What it does

Pooltrackr treats a pool business as more than a route. It covers service scheduling and technician jobs, repair work orders, quoting, and — unusually for this category — retail and point-of-sale, with inventory shared between what sells in the shop and what goes out on the trucks. Water testing results can be recorded and linked to customer records and product recommendations.

That combination suits a specific kind of company: one with a storefront selling chemicals and equipment as well as a service arm. Running those on separate systems means inventory that never reconciles and customers who exist twice, and a single platform removes a persistent administrative problem that route-only software does not address at all.

Who it suits

This is for a pool business with more than one revenue channel.

  • Companies with a retail shop as well as a service route.
  • Businesses doing significant repair and equipment installation work.
  • Operations that want shared inventory between shop stock and van stock.
  • Owners tired of reconciling a point-of-sale system against service software.

Where it falls short

Breadth across retail and service means less depth in pure route work.

  • Route optimisation and route-specific features are less developed than the route-first platforms.
  • Chemistry dosing automation is not at the level of the specialists, so chemistry-heavy route businesses gain less.
  • Per-user pricing adds up across a larger technician team, where stop-based pricing might work out better.
  • The product's development and support have a strong non-US regional orientation, so check support hours and payment processing options for your market.
  • If you have no retail side, you are paying for the main differentiator and not using it.

Before you buy

Only shortlist this if you genuinely have retail or substantial repair operations. For a pure cleaning route, the route-first platforms will serve you better.

Confirm payment processing and support arrangements for your region before committing.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
