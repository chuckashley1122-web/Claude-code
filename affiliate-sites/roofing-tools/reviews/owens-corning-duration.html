Owens Corning Duration: what it does and where it falls short

The main alternative in mainstream architectural shingles, with a reinforced nailing strip as its signature feature.

Category
Material — Architectural asphalt shingle
Starting price
Material only, approx. $120–$170 per square (regional; varies with market conditions)
Best for
Residential re-roofs in high-wind areas, and contractors wanting an alternative to the market leader
Not a good fit for
Low-slope applications and budget three-tab replacements

What it does

Duration is a laminated architectural shingle whose distinguishing feature is a woven fabric strip reinforcing the nailing area. The reinforcement improves fastener holding and, with the shingle's sealant system, supports higher wind-resistance ratings when installed to the manufacturer's requirements — which is the practical reason contractors in windy regions specify it.

Like its main competitor, it is sold as part of a system with matching starter, ridge, underlayment and ventilation products, and backed by a contractor programme that lets qualifying installers offer extended warranty coverage including workmanship. The colour range and the blended granule appearance are often cited as reasons homeowners prefer it in a side-by-side sample comparison.

Who it suits

A sensible specification where wind performance and appearance are both being sold.

  • Re-roofs in high-wind regions where the rated wind performance is part of the conversation.
  • Contractors who want a credible second option to quote against the most common shingle in their market.
  • Homeowners choosing on appearance, where the colour blending wins a sample comparison.
  • Companies enrolled in the manufacturer's contractor programme and selling its extended coverage.

Where it falls short

The wind rating and the warranty both come with conditions attached.

  • Higher wind ratings depend on installing exactly as specified, including fastener count and pattern and the correct starter course. Sell the rating, then install casually, and the rating is meaningless.
  • Extended warranties require contractor qualification and system component installation — not simply using the shingle.
  • Pricing tracks the same volatile asphalt and freight markets as every competing shingle, so quote validity windows matter.
  • Regional availability of specific colours is inconsistent, and substituting late in a job creates an unhappy homeowner.
  • Steep-slope only. It solves nothing on a low-slope section, which still needs an appropriate membrane approach.

Before you buy

Read the fastening requirements for the wind rating you intend to claim and make sure your crews know them. This is the most common gap between the spec sheet and the roof.

Verify colour availability at your supplier for the whole quantity before presenting samples to a homeowner.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
