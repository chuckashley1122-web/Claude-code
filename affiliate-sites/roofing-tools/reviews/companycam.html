What it does

CompanyCam is photo documentation built for contractors: every photo is automatically tagged with project, location, timestamp, and user, then organized into a project timeline the whole company can see. Annotations, checklists, before-and-after comparisons, and shareable galleries make it the default record of what a roof looked like before you touched it.

Who it suits

Essentially every roofing company, but especially those in insurance work or facing callback disputes, where proving pre-existing condition is worth more than the subscription. It integrates with the major roofing CRMs rather than replacing them.

Where it falls short

Per-user pricing means it scales linearly with headcount, and subs or one-day helpers are awkward to license. It solves documentation only — it is not a CRM, estimator, or scheduler, so it is another subscription alongside your platform rather than a consolidation. Photo upload on weak rural cell service backs up and drains batteries. Its real weakness is cultural: the tool only works if crews actually take the photos, and enforcing that is a management problem the software cannot fix.
