What it does

Roofr pairs ordered roof measurement reports with a proposal builder, a CRM layer, and instant estimate tools you can embed on a website. Measurement reports come back from satellite or drone imagery, and the proposal templates produce a clean, good-few-options document a homeowner can accept and sign digitally. Subscription plans bundle a monthly report credit.

Who it suits

Small and mid-size residential roofers whose competitive edge is speed and presentation — getting a professional proposal in front of a homeowner the same day. Bundled report credits make the measurement cost predictable.

Where it falls short

The CRM and production side is thin next to JobNimbus or AccuLynx, so growing companies often end up running Roofr alongside another system. Measurement turnaround is usually fast but not instant, and complex or heavily shaded roofs come back with errors often enough that you must check every report. Report credits beyond the bundle are billed individually and add up in a busy month. Commercial and low-slope work is outside its comfort zone.
