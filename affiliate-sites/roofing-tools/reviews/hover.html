What it does

Hover turns a set of smartphone photos into a dimensioned 3D model of a house, producing roof and siding measurements plus a visualizer that lets a homeowner see real product colors on their own home. The sales value is immediate: the customer stops imagining and starts choosing.

Who it suits

Exterior contractors selling roofing alongside siding, windows, or trim, and sales teams whose close rate depends on helping a homeowner visualize the result. Capturing photos yourself also avoids the stale-imagery problem of aerial providers.

Where it falls short

Someone has to be on site and walk the property taking a specific photo sequence, which makes it useless for remote or pre-visit quoting. Capture quality drives model quality, so poor photos or tight lot lines produce bad models, and steep complex roofs still need verification. Per-property pricing gets expensive across many quotes, and the tiers that include full measurement detail cost more than the headline. Insurance adjusters accept it less universally than EagleView.
