What it does

JobNimbus combines a sales CRM with production management built around roofing's actual stages: lead, inspection, claim, approval, order, build, collect. Boards are visual and configurable, documents and photos attach to the job, and the automation engine moves work along and triggers tasks without someone remembering to. Supplier ordering integrations reach major distributors.

Who it suits

Roofing companies running both a sales team and a production department, especially those handling insurance work where documentation volume per job is high and nothing can be lost.

Where it falls short

Pricing is quote-only and has moved upward, with reports of significant renewal increases — get multi-year terms in writing. The configurability that makes it flexible also means a poorly planned setup becomes a mess that is hard to unwind, and most companies need outside help to configure it well. Reporting is weaker than the operational side, so owners wanting real margin analysis by crew or lead source will export data. Performance slows noticeably on jobs with very large photo sets.
