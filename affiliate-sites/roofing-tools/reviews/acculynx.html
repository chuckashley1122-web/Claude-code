What it does

AccuLynx is a roofing-specific platform covering CRM, estimating, aerial measurement ordering, supplier integration, labor and material job costing, production scheduling, and commissions. Its insurance claim tooling and the depth of its job costing are the reasons companies choose it over a general CRM, along with direct ordering from major suppliers.

Who it suits

Established roofing companies with dedicated administrative staff and enough volume that per-job margin tracking and commission automation pay for the platform. Companies doing heavy insurance restoration get the most from it.

Where it falls short

It is among the most expensive options in the category, quote-only, and generally sold on annual commitments — small companies will find it hard to justify. The feature surface is large enough that most teams use a fraction of it while paying for all of it. Onboarding is a multi-week project and the interface takes real training. Smaller shops consistently report it as heavier than their operation needs, and the mobile experience lags the desktop product.
