Electrical contractor software and test gear, compared

Electrical contracting splits into two very different businesses. One is service and small works, which runs on dispatch and fast invoicing. The other is project work, which runs on estimates, progress billing, change orders and job costing. Software that is excellent for one is often wrong for the other, and the first five entries here are deliberately spread across that divide.

The other five are test instruments, where the buying decision is usually about how much measurement capability you need rather than brand. Each page covers what the product does, who it suits, and where it falls short.

How this list is put together

Descriptions come from vendor documentation, published pricing pages, instrument datasheets and user manuals. Where a vendor quotes per company rather than publishing rates, the table says so instead of guessing.

The list is built around the split in this trade:

  • Two service-oriented platforms, for companies whose work arrives as calls.
  • Three project and construction-oriented platforms, for companies whose work arrives as contracts.
  • One general-purpose bench-grade multimeter, as the instrument most decisions start from.
  • One insulation tester and one clamp meter, covering the two measurements a service electrician most often lacks.
  • One circuit analyser and one non-contact measurement tool, both bought to cut diagnostic time.
  • No product was included or excluded because of an affiliate relationship.
