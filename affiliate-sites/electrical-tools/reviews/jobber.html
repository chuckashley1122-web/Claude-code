What it does

Jobber runs requests through quotes, scheduling, job tracking, invoicing, and online payment with a client hub and automated quote follow-up. Its trade-agnostic design suits electricians whose work spans service calls, remodels, and small installation projects.

Who it suits

Small electrical outfits wanting solid quoting and billing without a heavy implementation, and shops with a mixed work type that resists a service-only platform.

Where it falls short

No electrical-specific depth at all — no flat-rate pricebook with assemblies, no takeoff, no material list generation from a plan. Project management for anything multi-phase is thin. Live dispatch beyond a few trucks is awkward. Per-user pricing on upper tiers escalates and the entry tier's user cap pushes most real crews up a tier immediately.
