What it does

Knowify targets contractors doing contract and commercial work: bid building from labor and material assemblies, progress and AIA-style billing with schedules of values, change order tracking, real-time job costing against budget, time tracking with certified payroll support, and tight QuickBooks integration.

Who it suits

Electrical contractors doing commercial and public work where AIA billing, retainage, change orders, and prevailing wage reporting are daily requirements — the exact functionality residential service platforms lack entirely.

Where it falls short

It is poorly suited to high-volume residential service dispatch: the scheduling and dispatch board are weak, and there is no flat-rate pricebook or membership machinery. Pricing is well above general small-business tools, which is hard to justify without real contract volume. The interface is utilitarian and the learning curve for bidding and AIA billing features is meaningful. The mobile app is limited compared with service-first platforms.
