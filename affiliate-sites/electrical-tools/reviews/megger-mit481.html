What it does

The MIT481 is a purpose-built insulation tester with selectable test voltages, automatic polarization index and dielectric absorption ratio calculation, timed test runs, and result storage with download for reporting. For anyone doing genuine insulation assessment rather than a quick pass/fail meg, the automated ratio testing is the whole point.

Who it suits

Contractors performing commissioning, acceptance testing, and formal preventive maintenance programs where PI and DAR values and documented trend data are contractual deliverables.

Where it falls short

It is a single-purpose instrument at a substantial price, so it sits in the truck unused between testing jobs — hard to justify unless insulation testing is a recurring revenue line. It is not a multimeter, so it does not reduce what else you carry. Interpreting PI and DAR results correctly requires training that the instrument does not provide, and misreading them is easy. Its voltage range still does not reach genuine medium-voltage requirements. Software for downloading results is dated, and calibration is an ongoing cost.
