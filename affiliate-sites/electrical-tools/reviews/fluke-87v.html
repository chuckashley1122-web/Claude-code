What it does

The 87V is the long-standing reference industrial multimeter: true-RMS AC measurement, 4.5-digit resolution, a low-pass filter for accurate readings on variable-frequency drives, temperature and capacitance functions, and a CAT III 1000V / CAT IV 600V safety rating. The low-pass filter and the durability are the reasons it stays in service for a decade.

Who it suits

Electricians and industrial maintenance techs who need one meter to trust for everything, particularly anyone working around motor drives where a non-filtered meter reports nonsense.

Where it falls short

It is expensive — several times the cost of meters that are perfectly adequate for residential work — and much of what you pay for is resolution and drive filtering many electricians never use. It is physically large and heavy for pocket carry, so most people end up also carrying a compact meter or a tester. There is no clamp, so current measurement above the fused input range needs a separate accessory. It has no data logging or connectivity, which newer meters at similar prices now include. Recalibration to keep it defensible is an ongoing cost.
