Housecall Pro: what it does and where it falls short

The fastest way for a small residential electrical shop to stop running on paper.

Category
Software — Field service management for small teams
Starting price
From approx. $79/month (published tiers)
Best for
One to roughly ten electricians doing residential service work
Not a good fit for
Commercial project work with progress billing

What it does

Housecall Pro handles the everyday service loop: book the job, schedule it, send the customer an automatic confirmation and an on-the-way notification, have the electrician build an estimate or invoice on a phone at the property, and take a card payment before leaving. Automated follow-ups and review requests run on their own, and recurring service plans cover maintenance or inspection agreements.

Behind that are customer records with job history, reusable price book items so quotes are consistent between electricians, GPS and time tracking, a QuickBooks sync, and reporting on revenue and job types. Everything important works from the mobile app, which is why small shops adopt it in days rather than months.

Who it suits

This is correctly sized for a small residential service business.

  • Owner-operators and small crews needing scheduling, invoicing and card payment working immediately.
  • Residential service and small works — fixtures, circuits, troubleshooting, device replacement.
  • Owners who want published pricing they can evaluate without a sales call.
  • Companies where the owner is still in the field and will use the app personally.

Where it falls short

Quick to adopt, and correspondingly shallow once the work gets complicated.

  • Job costing and reporting are thin. Revenue and job counts are easy; profit per job with burdened labour needs a spreadsheet.
  • Commercial and project work is a poor fit — no progress billing, no retainage, no change order workflow, no certified payroll.
  • Features are split across tiers and add-ons, so the headline price is rarely the working price.
  • Inventory and purchasing are basic; tracking material across several vans needs something else.
  • Card processing fees are part of the real cost — compare the effective rate against your current merchant account.

Before you buy

Price the tier containing your actual requirements at your real seat count, plus expected card volume. That is the comparison figure.

If you do any meaningful commercial project work, check whether you would need a second system for it before committing.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
