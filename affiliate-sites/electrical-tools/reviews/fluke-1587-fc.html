What it does

The 1587 FC combines a 50V to 1000V insulation resistance tester with a full-function true-RMS digital multimeter, plus wireless logging to a phone app for reporting. Having megohm testing and general measurement in one instrument means one tool on the belt when troubleshooting a motor or a suspect feeder.

Who it suits

Electricians doing motor work, cable fault diagnosis, and preventive maintenance programs where insulation trend data over time is the deliverable. Useful for anyone who must document test results for a client.

Where it falls short

As a dedicated insulation tester it is less capable than purpose-built megohmmeters — no polarization index or dielectric absorption ratio automation, and the top test voltage of 1000V is insufficient for medium-voltage work. Combining functions means compromise in both directions. It is expensive for a shop that only megs a motor a few times a year, where a cheaper standalone tester plus an existing meter costs less. The app connectivity is useful but occasionally flaky, and the overall package is bulky.
