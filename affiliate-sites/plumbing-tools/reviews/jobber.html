What it does

Jobber handles requests through quotes, scheduling, job tracking, invoicing, and payment, with a client hub for quote approval and online payment and automated follow-up on quotes left hanging. Being trade-agnostic helps plumbers who also do remodel or light HVAC work without fighting the software.

Who it suits

Small plumbing outfits wanting clean quoting and billing quickly, and companies whose work mix crosses trades. Good landing spot after abandoning something heavier.

Where it falls short

No real flat-rate pricebook with option presentation, no membership engine, and no plumbing-specific depth — it is a generalist. Live dispatch of more than a few trucks is awkward because the schedule view was not built for a dispatcher watching a board. Per-user pricing on upper tiers escalates, and the entry tier's user cap is low enough that most real crews start at least one tier up.
