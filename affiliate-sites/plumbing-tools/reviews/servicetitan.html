What it does

ServiceTitan runs the whole operation: call booking with recording, dispatch board, technician app with a flat-rate pricebook and option presentation, memberships, invoicing, payroll inputs, and reporting that ties revenue to lead source and technician. For plumbing specifically, the pricebook and the recurring-service machinery are the draw.

Who it suits

Residential service and drain companies past the point where the owner can hold dispatch in their head — typically twenty trucks and up, with real CSR and dispatch roles and documented processes worth enforcing.

Where it falls short

Cost is the headline problem: quote-only annual contracts with implementation fees, routinely landing far above what smaller shops expect. Implementation is a multi-week project requiring an internal owner, and the pricebook build alone is substantial. The configuration surface is large enough that mistakes produce confidently wrong reporting. New construction and commercial service contract workflows are materially weaker than the residential path it was designed around.
