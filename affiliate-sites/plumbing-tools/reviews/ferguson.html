What it does

Ferguson is the largest plumbing distributor in the United States, combining a national catalog with local branches for will-call pickup and jobsite delivery, dedicated contractor accounts with terms, quoted project pricing, and manufacturer reps who will actually come to your jobsite. For project work, the ability to get a bid package quoted and delivered on schedule is the product.

Who it suits

Any plumbing contractor needing same-day material, companies doing project work that requires quoted packages and staged delivery, and shops that value credit terms and a real account rep over transactional pricing.

Where it falls short

List pricing is uncompetitive unless you have negotiated contractor pricing, and what you pay depends heavily on your account size and your rep — small shops often do worse than online sellers on common items. Service quality varies significantly branch to branch, so the experience is local, not national. The online ordering experience lags pure-play e-commerce, and counter wait times at busy branches in the morning are a real productivity cost.
