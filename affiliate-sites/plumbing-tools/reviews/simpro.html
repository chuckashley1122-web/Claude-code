What it does

Simpro targets contractors running both projects and recurring maintenance, with multi-stage job costing, quoting from assembled cost catalogs, real inventory and warehouse management, subcontractor handling, planned preventive maintenance schedules, and detailed work-in-progress reporting.

Who it suits

Commercial and industrial plumbing contractors managing multi-phase projects, retentions, and maintenance contracts — the work where residential service platforms simply have no answer. Its inventory depth is a real differentiator.

Where it falls short

It is complex, and implementation is long and expensive — quote-only with substantial onboarding cost, measured in months rather than weeks for larger deployments. The interface is dense and training-heavy, with a steep curve for office staff. It is overkill for residential service shops, where lighter tools do the job better. Reports of support responsiveness varying by region come up consistently enough to check references locally before committing.
