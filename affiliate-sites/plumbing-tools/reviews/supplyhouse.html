What it does

SupplyHouse is an online-only distributor of plumbing, heating, and HVAC parts with deep catalog coverage, contractor pricing tiers, fast standard shipping, and a return policy that is unusually easy for the industry. Technical specification detail on listings is better than most, which makes it practical for identifying obscure repair parts.

Who it suits

Shops restocking trucks in bulk, anyone hunting a discontinued or unusual part, and companies in areas where the local supply house has thin inventory. Useful for planned work where shipping time is not a constraint.

Where it falls short

It is shipping-dependent, which makes it useless for the emergency call where you need the part in twenty minutes — a local counter still wins on same-day. There is no will-call, no counter relationship, and no local rep to lean on for a warranty problem or a job-specific question. Pricing beats local on many items but not all, so it pays to compare. Large or heavy items carry freight costs that erase the savings, and stock-outs on popular parts do happen.
