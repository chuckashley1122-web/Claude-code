ServiceM8: what it does and where it falls short

Priced by jobs instead of seats, which changes the maths for a small crew doing higher-value work.

Category
Software — Job management for small service businesses
Starting price
From approx. $29/month, priced by job volume rather than users
Best for
Very small plumbing businesses with low to moderate job volume
Not a good fit for
High-volume shops — per-job pricing works against you

What it does

ServiceM8 handles quotes, job dispatch, job records with photos and notes, custom forms and checklists, invoicing and payments. Its pricing model is the distinguishing feature: you pay for a bundle of jobs per month rather than per user, so an owner with a couple of technicians and an office helper is not penalised for putting everyone in the system.

The mobile app is the strongest part — it is designed for a technician to run the whole job from a phone, including capturing signatures and completing compliance forms, and it works sensibly offline before syncing. Custom form building is unusually good for a product at this price, which matters for plumbers who need repeatable job sheets, backflow forms or safety checks attached to the record.

Who it suits

This suits the small operator who wants structure without a per-seat bill.

  • One to three van operations with moderate job counts and higher-value tickets.
  • Businesses that need custom forms or compliance paperwork attached to every job.
  • Owners who want everyone — including part-time office help — in the system without extra seat costs.
  • Technicians working in areas with patchy signal, where offline capability matters.

Where it falls short

Job-based pricing is the reason to choose it and the reason it stops fitting.

  • High job volume makes it expensive. A drain-cleaning operation doing many small jobs a day will pay more than it would on a per-seat platform.
  • It is Apple-centric historically, and the mobile experience is strongest on iOS — check your fleet's devices before committing.
  • Reporting and business analytics are limited. It manages jobs well and tells you little about your business.
  • Membership and recurring service plan handling is weaker than platforms built around maintenance revenue.
  • The add-on model means functionality you assumed was included often sits behind a separate paid add-on.

Before you buy

Count your monthly job records honestly — including every small call — and price the job bundle you would need. Compare that against per-seat alternatives at your headcount.

Confirm which devices your technicians carry. The pricing advantage is wasted if the app experience is poor on their phones.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
