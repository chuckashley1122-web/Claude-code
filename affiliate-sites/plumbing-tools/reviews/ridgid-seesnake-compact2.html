What it does

The Compact2 is a self-contained push camera reel for 1-1/2-inch to 8-inch lines, pairing a durable pushrod with a self-leveling or standard camera head and pairing to RIDGID monitors for recording. It is small enough to carry into a crawlspace and tough enough to survive daily service work, which is why it is a default in residential drain work.

Who it suits

Plumbing companies doing regular drain diagnostics and sewer inspections, where showing a customer the actual root intrusion closes the job. Parts availability and service support for RIDGID are genuinely better than for off-brand alternatives.

Where it falls short

It is expensive — several times the price of import cameras that are adequate for occasional use — and the monitor is often a separate purchase, so the real all-in cost exceeds the reel price. Pushrod reach is limited compared with larger reels, so long mainline runs need a bigger machine. Pushrods do eventually kink or fail and replacement is costly. It does not locate: pairing with a sonde and a separate locator is another purchase. Cleaning it after a sewer job is unglamorous and unavoidable.
