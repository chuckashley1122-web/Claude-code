Plumbing business software and supply, compared

A plumbing company spends money in two places that decide whether it makes any: the software that books and bills the work, and the equipment that lets a technician finish a call in one visit. This site covers five of each.

The write-ups are organised the same way throughout: what the thing does, the kind of shop it suits, and where it falls short. That last section is the point. Every product here has a real weakness, and knowing it before you buy is worth more than another list of features.

How this list is put together

Descriptions come from vendor documentation, published pricing pages, equipment manuals and specification sheets. Where a vendor quotes per company rather than publishing rates, the table says so instead of inventing a number.

The mix was chosen around the decisions a plumbing contractor actually has to make:

  • Five office platforms spanning a single van up to a multi-crew company.
  • One inspection camera system, because diagnosis you can show the customer changes the job.
  • Two drain machines at different sizes, since one machine does not cover both a kitchen line and a main.
  • One press tool system, which is mostly a labour-cost decision rather than a tooling one.
  • One online supply channel, as the alternative to a trip to the counter.
  • No product was included or excluded because of an affiliate relationship.

What we do not publish
