What it does

JobLink is Fieldpiece's wireless probe ecosystem — pressure, temperature clamp, psychrometric, and vacuum probes that report to a phone app or a Fieldpiece meter instead of a hose-bound manifold. You can leave probes on a system, close the panel, and watch readings stabilize from outside, which changes how charging and airflow work actually gets done.

Who it suits

Service techs who charge and commission systems regularly, and anyone feeding a diagnostic platform such as measureQuick. Buying probes individually lets a tech build the kit over a few pay periods rather than in one purchase.

Where it falls short

Per-probe pricing makes a complete kit expensive once you total it up, and you need several probes before the workflow advantage appears. Battery management becomes a daily chore across a full set. Connection dropouts happen, usually at the worst moment, and reconnecting mid-measurement is annoying. The probes are small and genuinely easy to leave behind on a roof — plan on losing some. Mixing brands is limited, so you are largely committing to one ecosystem.
