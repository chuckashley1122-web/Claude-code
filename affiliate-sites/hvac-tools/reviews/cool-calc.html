What it does

Cool Calc is a browser-based Manual J load calculation tool that lets you trace a house footprint over satellite imagery, set construction assumptions, and produce an ACCA-approved room-by-room load report. It is widely used to satisfy permit and rebate requirements that demand documented sizing rather than a rule-of-thumb tonnage.

Who it suits

Residential replacement contractors in jurisdictions or rebate programs that require a Manual J, and any shop that wants to stop sizing equipment off the old unit's nameplate. The free tier makes it easy to try on a real job before committing.

Where it falls short

Satellite tracing is only as accurate as the imagery and your care with it, and a sloppy envelope produces a confidently wrong load. Construction defaults need real understanding to set properly — garbage assumptions in, authoritative-looking report out. It handles straightforward single-family homes well but gets frustrating with complex geometry, additions, and multi-zone designs. Manual D duct design is not its strength, so you may still need a second tool.
