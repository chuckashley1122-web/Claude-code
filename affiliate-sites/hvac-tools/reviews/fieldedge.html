What it does

FieldEdge targets HVAC and plumbing service companies with a dispatch board, a technician app with customer equipment history, flat-rate pricebook support, and a service agreement module built for recurring maintenance plans. Its QuickBooks integration is deeper than most, which matters for shops whose bookkeeper is not going to change systems.

Who it suits

Mid-size residential service companies with a real maintenance agreement base, where tracking renewal dates, covered equipment, and agreement profitability is a core business function rather than an afterthought.

Where it falls short

Pricing is quote-only with setup fees, so comparing it against published competitors takes a sales call you may not want to take. The product has accumulated interface inconsistencies over the years, and some workflows still feel like separate applications bolted together. Marketing attribution and customer-facing booking are thinner than at ServiceTitan, so shops that want to run lead-source reporting will find gaps. Reports of slow support response during peak season come up often enough to plan around.
