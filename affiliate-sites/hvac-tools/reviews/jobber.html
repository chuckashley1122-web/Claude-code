Jobber: what it does and where it falls short

Built around the quote, which makes it a good fit for install-and-project work rather than pure demand service.

Category
Software — Quoting, scheduling and invoicing
Starting price
From approx. $39/month (published tiers)
Best for
Small trades businesses where quoting volume matters as much as dispatch
Not a good fit for
High-volume demand-service dispatch operations

What it does

Jobber organises work as requests, quotes, jobs and invoices, and moves each one along that chain. Quotes are its strongest area: templated line items, optional add-ons the customer can approve themselves, online acceptance with a signature, and automatic nudges when a quote goes quiet. Approved quotes convert straight into scheduled jobs.

Around that sit a drag-and-drop calendar, route optimisation, a technician app with forms and photos, recurring job support for maintenance visits, a self-service client hub where customers see appointments and pay invoices, and automated invoicing with payment reminders. The QuickBooks Online sync is well regarded and covers customers, invoices and payments.

Who it suits

Jobber suits trades businesses where winning the quote is the bottleneck.

  • Shops doing a mix of service and installs, where a clear quote with options wins work.
  • Small teams that value a tidy, quick interface over configurability.
  • Businesses already running QuickBooks Online that want the bookkeeping to stop being manual.
  • Multi-trade operations — it is not HVAC-specific, which helps if you also do plumbing or electrical.

Where it falls short

Being trade-agnostic is the trade-off: nothing in it is shaped specifically around HVAC.

  • No HVAC-specific equipment modelling. Tracking serial numbers, refrigerant charge, model histories and warranty dates per unit means bending custom fields to the task.
  • Dispatch is adequate rather than strong. A high-volume demand-service operation moving many calls per day will find it less capable than a purpose-built dispatch board.
  • Maintenance agreement handling is basic compared with platforms that treat memberships as a revenue product, with renewal tracking and automatic billing.
  • Reporting covers the essentials but stops short of the job-costing and marketing-attribution depth larger shops want.
  • Per-user pricing on lower tiers caps how many people can be in the system, which bites sooner than expected once you add subcontractors or seasonal help.

Before you buy

Work out your split between quoted install work and unscheduled demand service. If you are mostly reactive service with high daily call volume, test the dispatch board hard before committing; if you are quoting replacements, Jobber's strengths land where you need them.

Check the seat limits on the tier you are pricing. Upgrading purely to add a user is a common and avoidable surprise.

Check the current price and terms directly with the vendor. Pricing, tiers and stock change constantly, and the vendor's own page is the only reliable source.
