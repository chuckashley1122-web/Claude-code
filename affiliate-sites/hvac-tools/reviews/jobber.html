What it does

Jobber handles requests, quotes, scheduling, job tracking, invoicing, and payments, with a client hub customers use to approve quotes and pay online. It is trade-agnostic by design, so an outfit doing HVAC plus light plumbing or appliance work does not have to bend the software to fit. The quote templates and automated follow-up on unapproved quotes are the standouts.

Who it suits

Small to mid-size shops that want clean quoting and billing without a long implementation, and companies whose work mix crosses trade boundaries. It is also a reasonable landing spot for a shop that tried a heavier platform and abandoned it.

Where it falls short

Because it is built for many trades, it lacks HVAC-specific depth: no real flat-rate pricebook with equipment options, no load-calculation integration, and no membership or maintenance agreement engine comparable to purpose-built HVAC platforms. Dispatching more than a handful of trucks in real time is awkward — the schedule view was not designed for a dispatcher watching a board all day. Per-user pricing on higher tiers adds up quickly, and the entry tier's user cap is low enough that most real crews start a tier up.
