# Gatekeeper questions (calls)

Source basis: video 1:38:45-1:44:38 as summarized in the playbook (line 70-71
"Power dialer and gatekeeper questions / Calling setup and honest introduction") and
line 308. The presenter's exact gatekeeper wording was not supplied; the questions
below are CA&J's honest version, not a transcript.

## Principles

1. **Honest introduction.** Say who you are and the business you represent:
   "Hi, this is Chuck with CA-J Enterprises."
2. **No false familiarity.** Never imply you know the owner, spoke before, or were
   referred, unless that is true and recorded.
3. **Ask who handles after-hours calls.** That person is the right contact.
4. **Offer one specific available time** taken from the real demo calendar, not a
   vague "sometime this week".
5. **No invented anchors.** No "normally" figures, no discounts, no deadlines, no
   claims about other clients' results.
6. **Pricing questions** get the meeting line and the booking page
   https://ca-jenterprises.com/ai.

## Questions

- "Who handles your after-hours calls?"
- "When someone calls after you close, where does that call go today?"
- "Is [owner or manager name, if verified] the right person to talk to about that, or
  is there someone else?"
- "Would [specific available time] work for a short walkthrough?"
- "Is there a better time or a direct line for them?" (record only what they offer
  freely; never pressure for personal numbers)

## If the answer is no

Thank them, ask whether they would prefer not to be called again, and record the
opt-out or "Not now" in the prospect row.
