# Discovery questions (sales meeting, first two minutes)

Source: playbook line 188 (step 20). Ask in this order, record the answers in the
opportunity notes, and do not claim that calls are being missed without evidence.

## First two minutes

1. "What happens when someone calls after the office closes?"
2. "Which questions interrupt you most?"
3. "Are you looking for confirmed bookings or callback requests?"

## Outcome question

4. Record the answer as one of: **confirmed bookings**, **callback requests**, or
   **unknown**, together with any constraint the owner names. This decides whether the
   assistant may create confirmed appointments or only requests (see the intake form,
   `booking_permission`).

## Meeting rules

- Never assert that the business is losing calls or revenue; ask, and record what the
  owner says.
- The demo shown in minutes 2-8 is labelled as a demonstration (source line 190).
- Terms are discussed by Chuck in the meeting only. No figures go into any follow-up
  email, DM, chat, or AI reply (MEETING-FIRST).
