## 09 Calculate client break even
Build addition. Record service fee, separate media spend, software or usage extras, average contribution per completed sale, lead to held appointment rate and held appointment to sale rate. Contribution means revenue less variable delivery costs. For real estate, include the client’s actual commission split and transaction costs; do not assume the video’s commission example is net profit.
Monthly acquisition cost = monthly equivalent service fee + media spend + separately charged tools. Required completed sales = round up(acquisition cost ÷ contribution per sale). Required held appointments = required sales ÷ close rate. Required leads = required held appointments ÷ lead to held appointment rate.
Illustration only: $1,300 service + $1,500 media = $2,800 per month. At $3,000 contribution per sale, 25% held appointment close rate and 40% lead to held appointment rate, the approximate break even planning requirement is one sale, four held appointments and ten leads. These inputs are assumptions, not CA-J results.
## 10 Check agency margin

## 33 Create unbooked lead follow up
Workflow 02-Unbooked: enter only qualified leads without an active relevant appointment. Send an immediate permitted booking acknowledgment and assign a call task due within five minutes during staffed hours. After hours, queue the call for the next staffed window. Build addition timing: after two hours, send one permitted reminder if still unbooked and no reply; after one day, send one email follow-up; after three days, assign a manual review task and end the sequence. ## 34 Create booked appointment follow up
Workflow 03-Booked: trigger on the selected calendar’s new booking event. Save appointment ID and time; move to Booked; stop Workflow 02; send meeting details and a confirmation request. Schedule reminders 24 hours and two hours before the appointment. Skip reminder windows already passed, so a near-term booking does not receive stacked reminders.
## 35 Handle confirmation and schedule changes
When a reply clearly confirms the active appointment, mark Confirmed and send the pre-call asset once. Ambiguous replies create an owner task. Do not automatically cancel an unconfirmed appointment. Cancellation stops that appointment’s reminders and offers a reschedule route. Rescheduling clears old waits and schedules reminders against the new appointment ID or time.
## 36 Handle attendance and no shows
Workflow 04-Outcome: after the meeting, require a real attendance outcome from the salesperson or reliable meeting record. Attended creates a next action task; it does not automatically mean Won. No show sends one permitted reschedule message and creates a follow-up task. Lost stops sales nurture. Paid stops acquisition messaging and starts onboarding only after payment verification.
