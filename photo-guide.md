> ⚠️ One restriction on all three: you cannot use a photo of an **identifiable person** to imply they endorse a product or service, and you cannot resell the photo itself as stock. For directory hero images of businesses, you're well inside the allowed use.

> ⚠️ Do **NOT** scrape images from each company's own website unless you have written permission — those are copyrighted by the business owner. Stick to the three sites above.

## Updating photos in SmartDirectoryAI

Based on the admin panel screenshots you sent. The labels may differ slightly by version.

### Step 1 — Prep the image
- **Listing card image** (the photo we're replacing): aim for **1200×800 px**, ~300 KB, JPG.
- **Hero / header image** (different field, full-width): **1920×700 px (5:1 ratio), under 1 MB**, JPG.
- Compress with https://tinypng.com or https://squoosh.app before uploading — keeps page load fast.

### Step 2 — Upload to Media Storage
1. In the left sidebar click **Media Storage**.
2. Click **Upload** (or drag the file in).
3. Rename it something descriptive, e.g. `sander-resources-hero.jpg`, so it's easy to find later.

### Step 3 — Replace the photo on a listing
1. Left sidebar → **SmartDirectoryAI** → **Listings** (or **Businesses**).
2. Click the listing you want to update (e.g. *Sander Resources*).
3. Find the **Featured Image** / **Main Image** / **Cover Photo** field.
4. Click the existing image's **×** to remove, then click **Select from Media Storage** (or **Upload**) and pick the new file.
5. Click **Save** at the bottom of the listing.
6. Open the public page in a new tab and hard-refresh (**Ctrl+F5** / **Cmd+Shift+R**) to confirm.

### Step 4 — Repeat for all 9 listings
Do them one at a time so you can verify each one. Budget ~5 minutes per listing including image picking.

### Tip — bulk uploads
If SmartDirectoryAI has a **CSV import** under Settings → Import, you can update all 9 image URLs at once: export the listings to CSV, paste the new Media Storage URL into the `featured_image` column, re-import. Check their docs to confirm this is supported in your plan.
