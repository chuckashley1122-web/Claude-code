# Chuck's Daily Grind — audit checklist

Layers on `_shared/audit-checklist.md`. Run the shared list first.

## Health claims — any hit blocks the deliverable

- [ ] No metabolic, cardiovascular, cognitive, or energy benefit claimed
- [ ] No "healthier than", "clean", "detox", "supports", "boosts", "may help"
- [ ] No medical or dietary advice, including about caffeine
- [ ] No pregnancy, medication, or condition-specific guidance
- [ ] Claims stay on taste, aroma, body, and experience
- [ ] Caffeine figures, if stated, are sourced and given as approximate

## Product facts

- [ ] Every origin, farm, producer, altitude, varietal, and process fact traces to the approved catalogue
- [ ] No invented certification, cupping score, or award
- [ ] Tasting notes match the catalogue entry
- [ ] Price, availability, and shipping claims verified against the live store
- [ ] Missing facts are flagged placeholders, not filled gaps

## Content accuracy

- [ ] Brewing ratios, temperatures, grind sizes, and times sourced
- [ ] Technical claims survive compression into short-form intact
- [ ] No gatekeeping — no implication that expensive equipment is required
- [ ] Internal links point at pages that exist
- [ ] No named competitor disparaged

## Publishing and sending

- [ ] Marked DRAFT with the intended destination stated
- [ ] Nothing scheduled, queued, or posted by the agent
- [ ] Email segment named and reviewed
- [ ] Subject line and preview text checked for spam triggers and clickbait
- [ ] Named human approver identified

## Promotions

- [ ] Margin at the discounted price confirmed by a human
- [ ] Stock cover confirmed
- [ ] Shipping capacity confirmed
- [ ] Terms, dates, and exclusions unambiguous
- [ ] Success measure defined before launch

## Isolation

- [ ] Nothing sourced from CA-J Enterprises or CA-J Consulting
- [ ] No customer data of any kind in the input or output
- [ ] Credentials used are the dedicated Grind credentials
