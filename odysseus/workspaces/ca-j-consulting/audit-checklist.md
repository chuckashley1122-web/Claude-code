# CA-J Consulting — audit checklist

Layers on `_shared/audit-checklist.md`. Run the shared list first. Every item
here is blocking — an unchecked box means the content does not ship.

## Absolute prohibitions — any hit blocks the deliverable

- [ ] No approval, denial, or pre-qualification, stated or implied
- [ ] No guarantee of rate, amount, term, timeline, or outcome
- [ ] No specific rate or payment figure — including "for example" figures
- [ ] No individualized advice about a named or identifiable person
- [ ] No legal or tax advice
- [ ] No language implying the reader is likely to qualify
- [ ] No urgency or scarcity framing on a financial product

## Fair lending

- [ ] No reference to or inference about race, colour, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, or receipt of public assistance
- [ ] No product steering by neighbourhood, area, or demographic proxy
- [ ] Comparable information offered regardless of who is asking
- [ ] No example borrower described in a way that carries a protected-class signal

## PII

- [ ] No SSN, DOB, driver's licence, or government identifier anywhere in input or output
- [ ] No bank statement, tax return, credit report, or loan application content
- [ ] No borrower name paired with any financial detail
- [ ] Nothing was echoed back from a sensitive paste
- [ ] Any PII incident flagged for session review

## Required elements

- [ ] Disclaimer present, verbatim, at the end
- [ ] Framed as general education throughout
- [ ] Uncertainty stated where it exists
- [ ] Every factual claim sourced from the approved manifest
- [ ] Reader pointed to a qualified professional for their specific situation

## Compliance review

- [ ] Compliance-review agent run, findings attached
- [ ] Named human compliance reviewer signed off
- [ ] Sign-off recorded before delivery, not after
- [ ] Prompt version and model recorded

## Isolation

- [ ] Nothing sourced from CA-J Enterprises or Chuck's Daily Grind
- [ ] No cross-business audience, list, or context used
- [ ] Credentials used are the dedicated Consulting credentials
